An independent record shop in the heart of Glossop

Coda Vinyl opened in spring 2025 and lives in the Retail Arcade at Glossop Market Hall. It's run by Neil McDonald, once the guitarist in 90s Manchester band Puressence and also behind Vinyl Planet in Macclesfield, along with his partner Barbara.

The racks cover jazz to rock and everything in between: punk, reggae, indie, post-punk, prog, dance, alternative and pop. There's a full wall of new vinyl as well as crates of second-hand finds. Gift vouchers are available too, which is handy for buying for a collector.
